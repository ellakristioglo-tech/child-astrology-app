# Privacy and security incident response

Version: 26 August 2026

Incident owner: Ella Kristioglo. Backup owner: to be appointed before official launch.

1. Detect and record the time of awareness.
2. Contain the issue: disable the affected deployment/analytics feature, revoke exposed tokens and preserve proportionate evidence.
3. Identify affected data, children/users, vendors and whether data left a device.
4. Assess likelihood and severity of harm, including child vulnerability and profile misuse.
5. Document the decision in the breach register, including incidents not notified.
6. If the breach risks rights and freedoms, notify Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens without undue delay and, where feasible, within 72 hours. If high risk, notify affected people without undue delay.
7. Require any processor to provide facts and remediation without undue delay.
8. Fix, test, monitor and document lessons learned.

Emergency contacts and vendor escalation links must be kept outside the public repository.
